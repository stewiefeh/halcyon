House rules

The listening room contract

Phones away during playback. Talk between sides, not over them. Applause is permitted and encouraged after side twos. The host picks the volume; arguments are part of the event.

Reserving

How seats work

Eleven seats go fast. Call the shop or ask at the counter; we don’t take online reservations for listening nights because half the regulars refuse to use the internet and we love them.

Friday nights are for listening

Call the shop to grab a seat. Tea’s on, lights are low, phones are in the basket by the door.
