The story

The TT-500 was the receiver of the turntable world: not the most exotic name, but the one that showed up in every decent system between 1975 and 1980 and simply worked. This one spent its first life in a southeast Portland living room and its second in a garage, which is where garage finds usually go to die.

Instead it came to us with a dead motor bearing and twenty-eight years of dust. The motor came apart cleanly, the bearing got repacked, the belt got replaced with the correct profile, and the suspension was re-tuned until the plinth floated the way the factory intended. It now measures wow and flutter inside original spec, which is the kind of sentence that makes Marcus unreasonably happy.
