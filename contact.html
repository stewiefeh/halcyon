Getting here

Two blocks off the Burnside bridgehead, street parking on Ankeny and Grand. The #4 bus stops at the corner. Bike parking out front, obviously, it’s Portland.

What happens when you walk in

You’ll be greeted

By Rosa or June, offered tea or coffee, and then left alone. Hovering is against shop policy and shop personality.

You’ll be asked one question

“What do you listen to?” Answer it honestly and the next forty minutes get very good. Everyone’s taste is right for something on the floor.

You’ll get to listen

Anything can be hooked up. Bring a record or borrow one. There are two chairs, one beanbag, and a strict rule that you may stay as long as you like.

See you on Ankeny Street

Tuesday through Saturday, ten to six. Sundays noon to five. Mondays the bench dreams of capacitors.
