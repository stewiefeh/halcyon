The way we do it

Bought once, kept for decades

Every piece on the floor went through the bench first. No exceptions, no “as-is” bargains, no mystery hiss.

On the bench

Fully restored before sale

New belts and capacitors, deoxed every switch, stylus and drive aligned, then thirty hours of burn-in with our own ears on it.

Honest paper

Every flaw written down

Veneer chips, replaced knobs, non-original parts: it all goes on the tag. What you read is what you hear, at full volume.

On our floor

Play it before you buy it

Bring your own record or borrow one of ours. The listening room is open whenever we are, and no one will hover.
