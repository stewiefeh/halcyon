The crates

Six thousand records, three floors, one rule: nothing goes on the floor until we play it. Grades are honest, prices are fair, and the $5 crate is sacred.

Crate: New Arrivals

Restocked every Thursday morning. Roughly 200 records hit the wall weekly: collections, estate buys, and the boxes from Tuesday's trade-ins.

Crate: $5 and Under

The honesty crate. Play-graded, priced to move, and absolutely loved by somebody once. Some of the best records in the building live here.

Crate: Deep Cuts

Staff picks that aren't famous and should be. Rosa's shelf. If you buy one, she will talk to you about it, and you will not regret it.

How we grade

Play-graded, not eyeballed

Every record that hits the wall gets played on the shop system, front and back, before it gets priced. We grade the record, not the cover, and we tell you what we heard.

GradeWhat it means here
NMPlays clean. Maybe one tick on a bad night. The sleeve would pass a photo shoot.
EXLight surface sound under the music on quiet passages. Sounds great, not perfect.
VG+You can hear it has lived, especially between tracks. Still a good record.
VGLoud and proud with audible wear. Belongs in the $5 crate or priced like it.

Trade day

Bring us your crates

We buy records Tuesdays and Saturdays. No appointment needed for under fifty records; above that, call Rosa first so she can clear a table.

What sells

Jazz, soul, funk, reggae, 60s-70s rock, and anything local with a Portland address. Good copies of good records, that’s the whole secret.

What doesn’t

Sealed classical box sets from 1987, warped thrift-store specials, and anything with a name written on the label in ballpoint. We’ll tell you kindly.

How it pays

Cash or store credit, your call at the counter. Store credit runs 20% higher and nobody has ever been mad about that math.

Thursday is restock day

The new arrivals crate goes out at 10am and the serious collectors know it.
