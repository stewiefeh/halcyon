How a repair runs

No mystery, no surprise calls

Step 1

Intake

Bring it in or ship it to us. You get a ticket number and a technician’s name. The estimate visit is free and usually takes a day or two.

Step 2

The written estimate

What’s wrong, what it costs, what it’s worth. If the repair costs more than the unit, we say so plainly and hand it back with the diagnosis.

Step 3

The bench

Approved work happens in order. You get one honest progress call, not five vague ones. Parts substitutions get approved by you first.

Step 4

Burn-in & pickup

Every repair runs a minimum 24-hour burn-in on the bench before it goes home. You leave with a written work order and a 90-day warranty.
