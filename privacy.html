Privacy

Short version: this site collects nothing, stores nothing, and phones nobody. The longer version is barely longer.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising trackers, no cookies, no sign-in, no third-party embeds. The site is static files served by a content delivery network.

The forms

The forms on this site are demonstration forms. You can type into them and press send, and nothing leaves your browser: no data is transmitted, stored, or seen by anyone. They exist to show how the real shop’s forms would work.

The theme toggle

The lamp switch remembers your choice using your browser’s local storage on your own device. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data clears it.

Fonts and images

Fonts and artwork are served from this site itself, so your browser talks to exactly one server: ours. Nothing is fetched from a font CDN or an ad network.

If you contact the real shop

Halcyon Stereo is a fictional business created as a portfolio piece. On a real shop’s site, this section would describe how phone calls and emails are handled. As written: we don’t have a mailing list, we don’t sell information, and the only records kept are the repair tickets on work you brought in.

Questions

About this page or the site, use the contact page. Everything here is fictional and built as a design portfolio piece.
